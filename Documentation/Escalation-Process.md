# Escalation Process

Not every issue should be handled by the same person.

The escalation process ensures that incidents are handled by the appropriate level of technical expertise.

## Escalation Flow

```text
L1 Support
    ↓
Basic Checks
    ↓
Issue Resolved?
    ├── Yes → Close Incident
    │
    └── No
         ↓
L2 Cloud Engineer
         ↓
Advanced Troubleshooting
         ↓
Issue Resolved?
    ├── Yes → Close Incident
    │
    └── No
         ↓
L3 / AWS Specialist
         ↓
Deep Investigation
         ↓
Resolution / Vendor Support
